Game Loop:
The game starts on a Wednesday and lasts 5 days everyday you are given new events that happen on the train progressing the story and giving you info for the final night. You are also given moments to explore and find clues on your own.

Game Mechanics:
The game is about coming to your own conclusion on what happened to the victim and guessing at the end to see if you get it right.

Spend the week gathering info, and clues and seeing events unfold then making the final choice at the end.

Setting:
A murder takes place on a train, but no detectives are on board means the passengers will have to figure out the murder themselves. Just one issue now everyone is terrified of one another and start freaking out and killing each other as well.
